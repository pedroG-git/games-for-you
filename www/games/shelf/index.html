How to play

Drag an item into a cubby with a free spot. Put three equal items in one cubby and they are sold.

Fill all the orders at the top before the ⏱ time or the 👣 moves run out.

Small dots at the bottom of a cubby show that more goods wait behind the front row. Empty a front and the next row comes forward.

🔒 Locked cubbies open after the shown number of sales, or drop a key on them. Items in ice thaw with sales nearby (two crack stages). Glue holds an item until a sale happens next to it. A ? box shows its goods once touched.

Quick sales in a row build a combo. From 3 in a row you get extra time or moves.

Helpers are rare: ↶ undo, 💡 hint, 🔀 shuffle, 🧲 magnet (sells one set for you), ⏳ extra time or moves. Every 10 levels you win +1 of each; a won daily gives one.
