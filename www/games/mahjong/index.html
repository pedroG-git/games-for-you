How to play

Tap two equal tiles to take them away. Clear the whole board to win.

Only free tiles can be taken: nothing lies on top, and the left or right side is open. Locked tiles look a bit darker.

Any flower matches any flower, and any season matches any season.

Some tiles exist four times. Taking the wrong two can block you later — think ahead!

Fair play: every board can be cleared. Undo is free, hints never lead you into a dead end, and a shuffle always gives a board you can finish.

Quick matches in a row build a combo for extra points. Golden tiles give coins.
