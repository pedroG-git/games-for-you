Little games for Joana. Play them anywhere, even offline.

Download for Android joanas-games.apk

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file, joanas-games.apk.
  3. The first time, Android asks to allow installing apps from your browser. Tap Settings, turn it on, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. Done! 🍔

Updates

When there's a new version, the app tells you on its home screen. Tap Update and install it the same way. Your progress stays.
